# Listing Checklist

Print this page and fill it out for every item you list. Keep the completed sheets in a folder by your workspace.

**Item:** ________________________________ **Date:** ___________

## Before you buy

- [ ] I searched completed/sold listings for this exact item
- [ ] I found at least 3 recent sales at similar prices
- [ ] I checked current competing listings on eBay and Amazon
- [ ] I can test the item (it turns on / works / is complete)
- [ ] The item is legal to resell (not counterfeit, not recalled)
- [ ] I know the condition: new / like new / good / acceptable
- [ ] The item is small and light enough to ship cheaply

## Costs

- Item cost: $________
- Shipping supplies (box, bubble wrap, tape): $________
- Postage estimate: $________
- Platform fees estimate (eBay ~13% or Amazon referral fee): $________
- Total cost: $________

## Price math

- Planned sale price: $________
- Minus shipping: $________
- Minus fees: $________
- Minus item cost: $________
- Expected profit: $________
- Is profit worth your time? Yes / No

## Listing

- [ ] Photos taken (front, back, sides, flaws, labels)
- [ ] Title includes brand, model, size/color/condition
- [ ] Description states what works and what does not
- [ ] Description lists what comes in the box
- [ ] Condition stated honestly
- [ ] Measurements included (clothing, furniture, etc.)
- [ ] Shipping speed and cost stated
- [ ] Returns policy stated
- [ ] Keywords buyers would search for included

## After sale

- [ ] Item packed securely (wrapped, no empty space)
- [ ] Label printed and taped securely
- [ ] Shipped with tracking
- [ ] Buyer notified with tracking number
- [ ] Sale recorded in spreadsheet/notebook
- [ ] Profit calculated after fees and shipping
